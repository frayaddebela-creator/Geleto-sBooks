About Inkbound

Building a home for readers, one book at a time

📚 Our Story

Inkbound was born from a simple love of books and the desire to create a space where readers can discover, discuss, and share their passion for literature. Founded in 2024, we've grown from a small book club into a thriving community of thousands of readers worldwide.

We believe that every book has the power to change a life, spark an idea, or provide comfort in difficult times. Our mission is to connect readers with stories that matter.

🎯 Our Mission

To create the most welcoming and useful public bookshelf on the internet — a place where anyone can find their next great read, share their thoughts, and connect with fellow book lovers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload books to Inkbound?

Book uploading is available through our Admin Dashboard. After logging in with an admin account, you can upload book covers, add descriptions, and manage the entire catalog. Regular users can suggest books through our contact form.

Is Inkbound free to use?

Yes! Inkbound is completely free for readers. We believe in open access to literature and community discussion. Some premium features may be added in the future, but the core experience will always remain free.

Can I read books directly on the website?

Yes! Our online reader supports dark mode, bookmarking, and a comfortable reading experience. Simply click "Read Online" on any book's detail page to start reading. You can bookmark your page and resume reading anytime.

How do I save my favorite books?

After creating a free account, you can add books to your Favorites or Wishlist with one click. All your saved books appear in your user dashboard, accessible from any device when you're logged in.

How can I contribute to the community?

You can write reviews, rate books, participate in discussions, and recommend books to other readers. Active community members may be invited to become moderators or content curators.

Is my data secure?

We take privacy seriously. Your account information is stored securely, and we never share your personal data with third parties. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
